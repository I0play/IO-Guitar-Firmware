# Built-in curriculum

1. **Clean Notes & Timing** — open E, A2, D2, A2, open E at 60 BPM.
2. **Legato, Muting & Control** — normal attack, hammer-on / pull-off candidates, palm mute and slide candidate.
3. **Chord Timing & Clean Strings** — multi-string chord events.

The teaching model covers **where** (LED / finger), **when** (beat / onset + miss timeout), **what** (string + pitch), **how** (attack / sustain / muting / technique — honest about unknowns), and **retention** (adaptive guidance / repetition).
